# Inspire Match ATS — Client Demo Runbook
**Meeting:** Thursday, 12 November 2026
**Presenter:** Hire & Inspire by Christine
**Product:** Inspire Match ATS — AI-Powered Recruitment Platform
**Positioning:** Sales-led B2B SaaS. Pricing, modules and onboarding are tailored privately after discovery.
**Status:** Development / pre-launch; demonstrate only tested workflows.

## 1. Before the meeting — 20-minute smoke test
Use an authorized DEMO workspace with fictitious candidates, not real applicant data.
- [ ] Sign in and verify the correct workspace and permissions.
- [ ] Open Dashboard and Today. Confirm metrics and interview/task counts load.
- [ ] Open Jobs; use an existing demo vacancy. Confirm Job Details.
- [ ] Open Candidates; verify a demo candidate and its assigned job/application.
- [ ] Open Pipeline; move only a DEMO candidate, then restore original stage if appropriate.
- [ ] Open AI Shortlisting with a demo JD and demo CV. If model unavailable, switch to pre-approved screenshots rather than improvising.
- [ ] Open Interviews. Use **Open Smart Scheduling** at the top; inspect existing interview. Do not schedule a real candidate.
- [ ] Open Hiring Debrief; show scorecards and decisions on existing demo data.
- [ ] Open Offers/Approvals with test records; **do not reveal salary** to a hiring-manager audience.
- [ ] Open Reports and export only synthetic/demo data.
- [ ] Confirm Notifications Center opens. No need to send live email.
- [ ] Verify browser zoom 90–100%, screen sharing, and internet connectivity.

## 2. Recommended 15-minute demo flow
**0:00–1:30 — Problem and vision**
Recruiters shouldn't spend their day switching spreadsheets, messages and disconnected hiring stages. Inspire Match brings the recruitment lifecycle into one organized workspace, with AI to support — not replace — hiring judgment.

**1:30–3:00 — Dashboard + Today**
Show active jobs, recruitment overview, daily priorities and actionable shortcuts.

**3:00–5:00 — Jobs + Candidates**
Show the vacancy, linked applications, candidate profile, and notes. Explain company-scoped access.

**5:00–7:00 — Pipeline**
Show stage visibility from Applied through Screening, Interview, Shortlisted, Hired/Rejected. Highlight tracking and accountable decisions.

**7:00–9:00 — AI Shortlisting**
Show a prepared JD/CV comparison and criterion-level evidence map. State explicitly that AI evidence is generated and must be checked against the source CV; recruiter owns final decision.

**9:00–11:00 — Interviews + Debrief**
Show interview scheduling, conflict check, 1–5 competency ratings, supporting evidence and consolidated debrief. Smart Scheduling checks visible conflicts; **candidate self-scheduling links are not implemented**.

**11:00–12:30 — Offers + Approvals**
Explain configurable approval process. Use a non-sensitive demo offer; do not show confidential salaries to unauthorized viewers.

**12:30–14:00 — Reports + Notifications**
Show management reporting, CSV/PDF exports where available, and action-linked notifications.

**14:00–15:00 — Discovery + next step**
Ask about number of recruiters, monthly hiring volume, countries, required integrations, approval structure, data residency/security requirements, and onboarding needs. Offer a tailored demo/proposal — **no public pricing or live payment**.

## 4. Claims to avoid
- Do NOT say it is the first Egyptian ATS without independent market verification.
- Do NOT promise candidate self-scheduling, persistent company-shared Interview Kits, or mobile native apps; these are roadmap work.
- Do NOT claim AI scoring is independently verified or error-free.
- Do NOT claim all privacy/RLS scenarios were penetration-tested.
- Do NOT imply Vercel build success equals authenticated functional testing.
- Do NOT quote pricing, show plan tiers publicly, or offer live checkout.

## 5. Contingency plan
If sign-in fails: switch to screenshots of Dashboard, Jobs, Pipeline, AI Shortlisting, Interviews, Debrief and Reports, and schedule a technical walkthrough.
If AI times out: show a previously prepared, consent-safe demo output and explain it is an example.
If a module errors: move to the next step; do not troubleshoot production live in front of the client.
If a feature is requested but incomplete: “It is part of our roadmap; we can prioritize it based on your workflow during discovery.”

## 6. Follow-up after the demo
Send a short thank-you, summarize client pain points, agree next demo scope and decision-makers, then prepare a tailored commercial proposal separately.
